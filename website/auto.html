Android Auto integration

The wheel's HUD, on the car screen.

Connect the phone to Android Auto and the plugin appears in the car's app launcher. Open it for a large, driver-focused display of everything the wheel reports — designed to be seen in a glance, and to show nothing that invites interaction while driving.

What the car display shows

Large HUD-style readout

Battery percentage and voltage rendered big, so the state of the wheel is readable from the driver's seat.

Live metrics

Speed, temperature, power draw and wheel load, updating in real time from the EUC World service.

Connection status & wheel model

“Connected — InMotion V12”-style status line, so you know data is flowing.

Read-only by design

The Auto interface is built for minimal driver distraction: it displays the wheel, it doesn't ask anything of the driver.

Connecting

  1. Connect your phone to the car — wired or wireless Android Auto both work, on built-in displays and aftermarket head units.
  2. In Android Auto's app launcher, open the EUC World plugin entry.
  3. The HUD appears with battery, voltage, speed and temperature as soon as the background service is talking to the wheel.

Before you drive: start the plugin on the phone first (it runs as a foreground service and keeps running with the screen off), so the car display has live data the moment you open it.
